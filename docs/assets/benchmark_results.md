# ForecastForge benchmark

Generated by `python -m forecastforge.evaluation.benchmark`. Every number is
reproducible: the series come from fixed seeds and no model is tuned on the
scored folds.

* 5 synthetic series of 2000 business days, seeds 11, 23, 37, 53, 71
* horizon 1, 75 walk-forward folds per series (first training window 500 rows, step 20, gap 0)
* MASE is scaled by the one-step naive error
* models: naive, seasonal_naive, moving_average, drift, ets, arima, lightgbm

| Model | MASE | MAE | RMSE | sMAPE (%) | Directional accuracy (%) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| naive | 1.088 ± 0.141 | 1.004 ± 0.295 | 1.289 ± 0.373 | 0.81 ± 0.08 | 0.0 ± 0.0 |
| seasonal_naive | 2.549 ± 0.388 | 2.367 ± 0.751 | 3.014 ± 0.992 | 1.92 ± 0.12 | 48.5 ± 4.5 |
| moving_average | 3.160 ± 0.701 | 2.967 ± 1.133 | 3.746 ± 1.379 | 2.33 ± 0.20 | 46.4 ± 4.9 |
| drift | 1.104 ± 0.164 | 1.022 ± 0.318 | 1.314 ± 0.391 | 0.82 ± 0.09 | 52.0 ± 6.1 |
| ets | 1.089 ± 0.147 | 1.007 ± 0.302 | 1.300 ± 0.384 | 0.81 ± 0.07 | 50.9 ± 4.8 |
| arima | 1.087 ± 0.143 | 1.004 ± 0.297 | 1.288 ± 0.374 | 0.81 ± 0.08 | 54.1 ± 4.7 |
| lightgbm | 1.145 ± 0.172 | 1.062 ± 0.335 | 1.340 ± 0.381 | 0.85 ± 0.10 | 49.3 ± 4.4 |

Values are mean ± standard deviation across seeds. MASE is unit-free and
scaled by the one-step naive error on each fold's training window, so 1.0
is exactly the naive forecast: below 1 beats it, above 1 is worse than
doing nothing.

A model is only described as better when its mean MASE is at least one
naive standard deviation below the naive mean; anything closer is within
seed noise and is reported as a tie.

**No model beats the naive forecast.** Naive scores 1.088 ± 0.141 and nothing is more than one standard deviation below it; the closest is arima at 1.087, which is a tie. That is what a near-random-walk price path implies: there is no exploitable structure left to find, and every additional parameter mostly adds noise.
